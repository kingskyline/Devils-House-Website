Alpha Development 30/07/2026 - 08:35 AM

Development Update #2

King Skyline [DHC]

It's been another huge week of development on Cargo Router, and this update has been all about transforming the game from an early prototype into something that finally has its own visual identity.

Rather than simply adding more features, I've spent this update improving the overall presentation, polishing gameplay, and laying the groundwork for future content.

🎨 A New Visual Direction

One of the biggest changes this week was making the decision to completely rethink the look of the game. Originally I was trying to fake 3D depth inside a 2D game using gradient lighting and stretched sprites to create the illusion of deep loading chutes. While it worked... it never really looked right. The more I worked on it, the more it felt like I was fighting the engine instead of working with it.

So I scrapped the entire idea. Cargo Router is now embracing a much cleaner, arcade-inspired art style that looks sharper, performs better, and is far easier to expand in the future. Sometimes removing a feature is the biggest improvement you can make.

🏭 Introducing the Industrial Tunnel

Instead of individual loading pits, the sorting area now features a single industrial tunnel running across the bottom of the warehouse. As parcels reach their destination they now disappear underneath the heavy metal structure before being sorted, creating a convincing illusion of depth without relying on complicated lighting tricks.

✔ The conveyor belts have been redesigned with cleaner mechanical textures that give the entire play area a much more industrial appearance.

💡 Neon Arcade Style

The UI already had a futuristic industrial look, so the gameplay area needed to match. The warehouse now features glowing neon lane indicators that instantly highlight where each parcel should be routed.

Combined with the darker matte metal surfaces, the contrast makes everything much easier to read during fast-paced gameplay while giving Cargo Router its own distinctive sci-fi warehouse aesthetic.

✨ Bringing the Warehouse to Life

This update also introduces one of my favourite additions so far. The sorting tubes are now filled with glowing energy effects that slowly drift upwards through the warehouse.

Using custom particle effects and additive lighting, what originally looked like simple smoke has been transformed into vibrant streams of sci-fi energy that perfectly complement the futuristic warehouse setting. It adds a huge amount of atmosphere to every shift.

⚙️ Gameplay Refinements

Packages occasionally drifted slightly away from the centre of the conveyor during testing. After tracking the issue down to legacy movement code, the routing system was rebuilt around the exact positions of each loading bay.

✔ The result is tighter movement, with every parcel travelling perfectly through the centre.

📦 Built for Expansion

The new tunnel acts as a single foundation across the bottom of the screen. Whether I decide to introduce four, five, or more parcel lanes in future updates, the system is designed to scale.

Future-proofing now saves a lot of work later.

🖥️ A Much Bigger Main Menu

I've started expanding the main menu into something that feels like the front-end of a complete game. A brand-new warehouse background has been created, featuring a much larger industrial cargo facility. I've also begun work on a dedicated Roadmap panel to show upcoming features directly in-game.

🏗️ Keeping the Code Clean

One thing I'm really happy about is that these visual upgrades haven't required any major rewrites. Because the gameplay systems were built separately from the graphics, I was able to completely redesign the warehouse without breaking the sorting logic underneath.

The delivery system, scoring, collisions, and gameplay all continued working perfectly. It's a great reminder of why building clean, modular systems early on is worth the effort.

🚚 What's Next?

With the visual direction now locked in, the focus shifts back towards expanding gameplay. Over the next few updates I'll be working on:

  • 📦 More parcel varieties
  • 🚨 New warehouse events
  • 📈 Additional upgrade systems
  • ✨ More visual effects
  • 🔊 Improved audio and ambience
  • 🏭 New levels and layouts

Cargo Router is starting to feel less like a prototype and more like the arcade game I originally imagined. Every update gets us one step closer to an early public release, and I can't wait to share more as development continues.

Thanks for following the journey! 🚛📦
